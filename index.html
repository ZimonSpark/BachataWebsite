SBS no-profit association

SBS ry (Simon Bachata Sensual) is a no-profit association born to grow the Bachata Sensual community in Helsinki.

What started in the summer of 2025 as free outdoor classes in the park has become a community of dancers who meet every week to learn, connect and have fun together.

Every Friday SBS organises social Bachata parties, a place where everyone is welcome, from the very first steps to experienced dancers.

What is Bachata?

Bachata is the Dominican Republic's music of the people. Its name comes from an African word meaning "informal", once a put-down for anything outside upper-class taste. Born from bolero and son heard on AM radio, it took shape after 1962, when Radio Guarachita began recording its first artists. The güira, steel-string guitars and the requinto built its sound, and the 1990s added the driving mambo. Danced close, with hips, rotations and a tap, it remains the heartbeat of Dominican social life. It has later evolved into Bachata sensual fusion in Spain adding waves and new instruments.

The three pillars of Bachata

Social activity

Bachata is not meant to remain in the classes. You learn to prepare yourself for the real world, bachata parties are everywhere around the globe. With bachata sensual you'll be able to explore.

Conversation

Bachata is a conversation through body movements. To avoid boring monologues, leader and follower listen to each other. Only this way the conversation can be pleasant, and sometimes, even mindblowing.

Fun Fun Fun

Bachata is supposed to be fun all the way from the beginning. You don't win a medal to accomplish a move or coreo. Focus on connecting with whom you are dancing rather than looking good.
