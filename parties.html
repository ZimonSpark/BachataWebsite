Bachata Club Nights

SBS organises parties every Friday at Bachata Club Nights. Located in Vallila at Teollisuuskatu 9d, 00550 Helsinki. Well connected and located in a creative hub called OddSpace.

Bachata Club is the best place to dance bachata in Finland, with a powerful and highly tuned sound system, light shows, and a spacious venue curated in every detail. During the parties there are normally also art expositions in the venue for the enjoyment of the dancers.

You can find the ticket to the next event on the WeDance app or website at this link.

Come to enjoy a magical atmosphere!
